## Overview

This document proposes a systematic scheme for collecting information about "The Valiant Sixty" - a group of early Quaker missionaries who spread Quakerism throughout Britain and beyond in the 1650s and 1660s. - **Wikipedia Quality:** Not all Wikipedia articles are well-sourced
- **Language:** Some sources may be in older English
- **Bias:** Historical sources may have religious or political bias

### **Mitigation Strategies**
- Document all uncertainties explicitly
- Use multiple sources when possible
- Prefer official Quaker sites
- Note reliability levels for each data point
- Include "unknown" or "uncertain" markers where appropriate
