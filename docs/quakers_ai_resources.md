# Quakers and Artificial Intelligence: Public Resources and Activity

## Overview

Quaker communities worldwide have been actively engaging with artificial intelligence through educational programs, publications, organizational statements, and interfaith dialogues. This document summarizes the current landscape of Quaker-AI engagement.

---

## Major Organizations and Their Activities

### **1. Quakers in Britain (Britain Yearly Meeting)**

**Online Course: "Artificial Intelligence: What Does It Mean for Quakers?"**
- **Dates:** November 6-27, 2025 (4 sessions)
- **Location:** Online
- **Leader:** Kenneth Cukier, journalist and AI expert
- **Topics Covered:**
  - How AI works and its societal impact
  - Pros and cons of AI in society
  - Novel religious and spiritual questions raised by AI
  - Machine consciousness and ethical embedding
  - Practical issues in society
  - The future of AI and humanity
- **Website:** https://www.quaker.org.uk/events/artificial-intelligence-what-does-it-mean-for-quakers

---

### **2. Friends World Committee for Consultation (FWCC)**

**Position Statement on AI Usage (October 2025)**
- **Status:** "Holding position" (cautious, reflective stance)
- **Key Points:**
  - Acknowledges potential benefits (translation, grammar correction)
  - Expresses concerns about:
    - Environmental impact of AI systems
    - Data usage and privacy
    - Relational aspects of communication
  - Advises cautious use, especially where personal relationships are paramount
- **Website:** https://fwcc.world/fwccs-holding-position-on-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence/

---

**Clerk's Post: "Artificial Intelligence and Collaborative Wisdom" (August 2, 2022)**
- **Focus:** Threats AI poses to democracy, ecology, and economy
- **Emphasis:** Need for spirit-led discernment and action
- **Website:** https://quakerinstitute.org/clerks-post-on-artificial-intelligence-and-collaborative-wisdom/

## Key Themes in Quaker-AI Engagement

### **1. Ethical Discernment**
- Application of Quaker discernment processes to AI evaluation
- Use of queries to guide reflection
- Emphasis on communal decision-making

### **2. Quaker Testimonies**
- **Simplicity:** Does AI simplify or complicate life?
- **Peace:** Military applications and conflict resolution
- **Equality:** Who benefits? Who is left behind?
- **Integrity:** Transparency and honesty in AI systems
- **Stewardship:** Environmental impacts

### **3. Practical Applications**
- AI in Quaker education (Carolina Friends School example)
- AI for outreach and community engagement
- Translation and communication tools
- Administrative and organizational uses

### **4. Concerns and Cautions**
- Environmental impact of AI systems
- Data privacy and usage
- Relational aspects of communication
- Threats to democracy, ecology, and economy
- Military and autonomous weapons

### **5. Spiritual Questions**
- Machine consciousness
- What it means to be human in an AI world
- Ethical embedding in AI systems
- Spirit-led solutions

## Educational Resources

### **Queries for Reflection (from Western Friend)**
- Who primarily develops and operates this technology?
- Who benefits from it, and are these benefits broadly shared?
- Does the technology contribute to humanity's betterment or degradation?
- What are the social, personal, and environmental risks?
