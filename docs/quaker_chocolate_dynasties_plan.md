# Quaker Chocolate Dynasties Knowledge Graph Plan

**Date:** December 10, 2025  
**Project:** Knowledge Graph for Quaker Chocolate Dynasties (Cadbury, Rowntree, Fry families)

---

## Overview

This document outlines the plan for creating a comprehensive knowledge graph of the three major Quaker chocolate dynasties: Cadbury, Rowntree, and Fry families. The graph will cover their businesses, family relationships, contacts/networks, and philanthropic work, limited to 50 key entries total.

---

## Four Phases of Data Collection

### Phase 1: Businesses (12 entries)
Focus on chocolate manufacturing companies, factories, and key business milestones.

### Phase 2: Families (18 entries)
Focus on key family members across three generations, including founders, successors, and notable descendants.

### Phase 3: Contacts (10 entries)
Focus on business partners, inter-family marriages, and connections to other Quaker families and organizations.

### Phase 4: Philanthropic Work (10 entries)
Focus on charitable trusts, foundations, model villages, and social reform initiatives.

**Total: 50 entries**

## Knowledge Graph Structure

### Color Coding (Following Style Guide)

- **Gold:** Central figures (founders: John Cadbury, Joseph Rowntree, Joseph Fry) - `penwidth=3`
- **Light Pink:** Family members - `penwidth=2` (all Quaker)
- **Light Cyan:** Quaker business leaders and philanthropists - `penwidth=2`
- **Light Steel Blue:** Key business figures - `penwidth=2`
- **Light Coral:** Non-Quaker contemporaries (if any) - `penwidth=1`
- **Light Gray (Diamond):** Businesses, factories, organizations - `penwidth=2` (Quaker)
- **Orange (Diamond):** Key events and innovations - `penwidth=2`
- **Plum (Diamond):** Philanthropic organizations and trusts - `penwidth=2`

### Node Types

- **People:** Family members, business leaders, philanthropists
- **Businesses:** Companies, factories, products
- **Places:** Model villages, factories, locations
- **Organizations:** Trusts, foundations, movements
- **Events:** Key business milestones, innovations

### Edge Types

- **Family relationships:** father, mother, son, daughter, brother, sister, spouse
- **Business relationships:** founded, managed, worked_for, merged_with
- **Philanthropic relationships:** established, supported, funded
- **Social relationships:** friend, colleague, mentor, influenced_by
- **Location relationships:** located_in, built, created
